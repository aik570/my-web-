## Plan

1. **Logo (shots 02, 03, 07) is never generated by a video model.** It is drawn from the approved SVG: a stroke trace, then a fill, in SVG/CSS. In shot 07 it is the same SVG laid over a generated mountain plate with a matched grade. Until a logo is approved, the storyboard uses a marked **LOGO SLOT**.
2. **Opening (01–03) autoplays once**, about 4 s, then hands over to scroll. It can be skipped by scrolling or pressing a key, and plays once per session.
3. **Mountain + eagle journey (04–07) is scroll-driven** and shown as an **image sequence on canvas**, extracted from generated video. This is the provisional choice from Visual Identity §13.3, because scrubbing a `<video>` element forwards and backwards is unreliable, especially on iOS Safari. It will be tested against video scrubbing before committing (phase D/G).
4. **Continuity:** shots 04 → 05 → 06 → 07 are generated image-to-video, with **shared boundary frames**. The last frame of one shot is the first frame of the next, so cuts are invisible.
5. **No real-time 3D.**

## Shared visual rules

These follow Visual Identity §5–§7 and apply to every frame.

- **World:** high-alpine winter, dark granite/gneiss peaks, snow in couloirs, deep winter. No identifiable landmarks.
- **Light:** a single cold key light from the **upper left**, low sun about 15–25°. A warm champagne accent appears only on the logo and on one focal ridge edge.
- **Grade:**
  - Shadows Obsidian `#101419` / Deep Slate `#253746`
  - Midtones Steel `#647F93`
  - Highlights Ice `#F2F5F7`
  - Desaturated
- **Haze:** blue-grey, increasing with distance. Foreground, midground and background are clearly separated.
- **Eagle:** adult **golden eagle** (*Aquila chrysaetos*):
  - umber-brown plumage graded toward graphite, tawny-golden nape
  - gliding with 5–7 separated primary feathers per wing
  - flight vector **left → right with a slight climb**, never reversing between shots
  - fills 8–25% of the frame width
- **Banned:** lens flares, bloom, orange sunsets, sun disc in frame, saturated blue, fantasy spires, text in generated images, bald eagle, aggressive poses.

**Base prompt block** (prepended to every mountain/eagle prompt):

> Photorealistic high-alpine winter landscape, dark granite and gneiss peaks with snow in couloirs and on ledges, deep winter, cold directional light from upper left, low sun, natural atmospheric perspective with blue-grey haze increasing with distance, clear foreground, midground and background separation, colour grade: shadows deep slate #253746 and obsidian #101419, midtones steel blue #647F93, highlights ice white #F2F5F7, desaturated, premium landscape cinematography, anamorphic 2.39 feel, no text.

**Negative block** (appended to every prompt):

> no orange or pink sunset, no sun disc, no saturated blue sky, no lens flare, no bloom, no HDR halos, no fantasy spires, no floating rocks, no people, no buildings, no text, no watermark, no bald eagle, no cloned ridges, no smeared snow texture.

## Shots

### SHOT 01: Black / light
- **Composition / camera:** full-frame near-black Obsidian, static.
- **Light / material:** very fine, barely visible material grain (brushed stone) at 2–3% contrast. A single thin champagne `#B8A58A` light line, 1 px, appears horizontally at the optical centre and extends from 0 to about 40% of the width.
- **Start → end:** pure Obsidian → line drawn at 40% width, soft falloff at its ends.
- **Transition:** the line becomes the base line, the "horizon", from which shot 02's logo strokes rise.
- **Duration:** 0.8 s.
- **Format / use:** **SVG + CSS**, with the texture as a procedural SVG noise filter or a ≤15 KB tiled image. No generation needed.
- **Continuity:** line colour and position match the baseline of the logo in shot 02.
- **Storyboard frame:** none needed. It will be shown as an HTML mock-frame once the logo is approved.

### SHOT 02: Logo construction
- **Composition / camera:** logo centred, about 38% of viewport width on desktop and 72% on mobile, static camera.
- **Light / material:** a champagne light point travels along the logo outline (SVG `stroke-dashoffset`): the left flank, the summits, the right flank. Then the shape fills with Ice White. A brief champagne edge highlight on the upper-left edges echoes the key light.
- **Start → end:** horizon line → complete flat logo in Ice on Obsidian.
- **Transition:** the fill completes; the light settles on the left summit.
- **Duration:** 2.1 s; draw at `cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1)`, fill 400 ms.
- **Format / use:** **SVG/CSS from the approved logo file**. Exact geometry, nothing generated.
- **Continuity:** the same SVG appears in shot 07.
- **Blocker:** needs the approved logo.

### SHOT 03: STUDIO wordmark
- **Composition / camera:** STUDIO set in Space Grotesk, tracked (+0.42em), centred beneath the symbol (stacked lockup proportions), static.
- **Light / material:** letters fade in with a 12 px rise and 60 ms stagger. A 40 px champagne rule appears beneath.
- **Start → end:** symbol only → complete stacked lockup → scroll cue (a thin line plus "Scroll").
- **Transition to 04:** on the first scroll, the lockup scales down by 6% and dissolves (opacity 1 → 0) while the shot 04 plate fades up through haze. The logo "dissolves into the mist" rather than cutting.
- **Duration:** 1.1 s, then hold until scroll.
- **Format / use:** DOM text with self-hosted fonts already in the project, or outlined SVG text.
- **Continuity:** the same wordmark appears in shot 07.

### SHOT 04: Mountain world (mode A, Dark & Dramatic)
- **Composition / camera:**
  - Wide establishing shot, 35 mm, camera at ridge height.
  - Dark rock ridge foreground (bottom 20%), a massif of three distinct peaks in the midground, ranges dissolving into haze behind.
  - Large calm haze zone at the centre where the logo dissolved.
- **Camera move:** slow push-in plus 2° tilt up.
- **Light / atmosphere:** low cold raking light from the upper left catching the ridgelines; cloud sea in the valleys; one small restrained warm highlight on the left summit edge.
- **Start → end:** haze-filled frame (matching shot 03's dark field) → mountains resolved, push-in about 8%.
- **Transition:** the eagle enters frame left at the end of the range.
- **Scroll range:** 0–15%.
- **Format / use:** image sequence frames (from video), plus a single poster still for the fallback.
- **Continuity:** this frame defines the massif, light and grade used by 05–07.
- **Image prompt (storyboard frame):**
  > [BASE] Wide establishing shot at dusk-blue hour, camera at ridge height, 35mm lens. Foreground: dark wet granite ridge across the bottom fifth of the frame. Midground: a monumental massif with three clearly distinct snow-covered summits of different heights, the centre one highest but not dominant. Background: further ranges dissolving into blue-grey haze. A sea of cloud fills the valleys. Low cold raking light from the upper left catches the ridgelines; a single small restrained warm highlight on the left summit edge. The centre of the frame is a calm area of soft haze. Mood: silent, monumental, precise. [NEGATIVE]
- **Video prompt (phase D):** slow forward dolly and slight upward tilt toward the massif, clouds drifting slowly left to right, no camera shake. 8 s, silent.

### SHOT 05: Eagle flight
- **Composition / camera:**
  - Same massif, slightly closer (50 mm).
  - The golden eagle enters from frame left at mid-height, gliding left → right with a slight climb toward the gap between the centre and right summits.
  - The eagle fills about 15% of the frame width at its closest.
- **Light / atmosphere:** the eagle is lit by the same upper-left key; tiny specular highlights on the leading wing edge and golden nape; the underside in cool bounce shadow.
- **Start → end:** shot 04's end frame → the eagle at the right-centre, heading into the pass.
- **Transition:** the camera follows the eagle's vector into the pass, which becomes the first frame of shot 06.
- **Scroll range:** 15–45%.
- **Format / use:** image sequence with the eagle rendered **inside the plate**. A separate alpha layer only if testing shows it is needed.
- **Continuity:** same species, plumage, wing proportions, light and flight direction in every frame. An eagle reference sheet is generated first, and every eagle shot uses it as a reference image.
- **Image prompt:**
  > [BASE] Same alpine massif as the reference image, slightly closer, 50mm lens. An adult golden eagle (Aquila chrysaetos) glides from the left side of the frame toward the right, wings fully spread in a slight dihedral, five to seven separated fingered primary feathers on each wing, deep umber-brown plumage, tawny golden nape, matte feathers, lit from the upper left with cool bounce light on its underside. The eagle occupies about 15% of the frame width, at mid-height, heading toward the gap between the centre and right summits. Anatomically accurate, calm, controlled gliding. [NEGATIVE]
- **Eagle reference-sheet prompt (generated first):**
  > Adult golden eagle (Aquila chrysaetos) reference sheet on a neutral dark grey background, four views in a row: side glide, top-down glide, three-quarter underside, banking turn. Wings fully spread, five to seven separated fingered primaries, deep umber-brown plumage, tawny golden nape, matte feathers, single cold key light from the upper left, consistent proportions in every view, anatomically accurate, photorealistic, no text.

### SHOT 06: Scroll-driven journey (mode C, Bold & Immersive)
- **Composition / camera:**
  - Camera moves through the pass between the centre and right summits.
  - Rock faces rise on both sides (35 mm, slightly low angle); haze layers part as the camera advances.
  - The eagle stays ahead of camera, smaller (8–10% width), continuing left → right in a slow banking climb.
- **Light / atmosphere:** strongest contrast of the sequence; light shafts through a cloud break, deep shadow on the near walls. No warm light except a sliver on one ridge edge.
- **Start → end:** the pass entrance → emerging from the pass into open air above the clouds, facing the final composition.
- **Transition:** the camera clears the pass, and the frame opens into shot 07's composition.
- **Scroll range:** 45–85%.
- **Format / use:** image sequence (the longest segment; the most frames).
- **Continuity:** the eagle's vector and scale shrink smoothly; the light direction stays fixed.
- **Image prompt:**
  > [BASE] Camera flying through a narrow mountain pass between two steep snow-streaked granite faces, 35mm lens, slight low angle. Layers of haze ahead parting, a cloud break letting soft light shafts fall diagonally from the upper left. Far ahead, the same adult golden eagle, small (about 8% of frame width), banking gently to the right and climbing. Strong contrast, deep shadow on the near rock walls, calm and immersive, sense of immense scale. [NEGATIVE]

### SHOT 07: Final brand reveal (mode B, Clean & Epic)
- **Composition / camera:**
  - Above the cloud sea, facing the massif from a distance (85 mm compression).
  - The three summits sit in the lower 45% of the frame; a large calm sky and haze area above for the lockup.
  - The camera decelerates to a stop.
  - The eagle is a distant silhouette exiting frame right, or gone.
- **Light / atmosphere:** the calmest, brightest frame of the sequence; diffuse high-key overcast with a gentle upper-left direction; Mist and Ice dominant.
- **Logo integration:**
  - The approved **SVG stacked lockup** resolves in the calm area above the summits, Ice White, with a 1 px champagne edge accent on the upper-left edges.
  - Its three peaks align optically with the three real summits below: the logo "echoes" the mountain.
  - STUDIO appears last.
- **Start → end:** shot 06 exit → logo and STUDIO fully visible, held for the last 5% of scroll, then the page releases.
- **Scroll range:** 85–100%.
- **Format / use:** a final still plate (AVIF) with the SVG logo overlaid in the DOM. This frame is also the **reduced-motion and failure fallback**.
- **Continuity:** the summit spacing in this plate is generated or retouched to match the approved logo's peak spacing.
- **Image prompt:**
  > [BASE] Serene, high-key alpine composition above a sea of clouds, 85mm compressed perspective, the same massif with three distinct snow-covered summits occupying only the lower 45% of the frame, the centre summit highest. The upper 55% is calm, soft, pale grey-blue sky and haze with no detail, reserved for a logo. Diffuse overcast light with gentle direction from the upper left, Mist grey and ice white dominant, quiet and confident. [NEGATIVE]
