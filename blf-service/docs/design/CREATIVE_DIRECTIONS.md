# Creative directions — Stage 04B

Stage 04B · 2026-10-27 · Status: **for review, no direction approved yet**

This stage resets the creative ambition of the homepage. It has three parts:

1. three genuinely different directions;
2. a working prototype of the hero, intro and first three scroll scenes for each;
3. side-by-side screenshots of the real prototypes.

Nothing here is the final website. The Stage 04 Home prototype (`src/`) stays
the baseline and is untouched.

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Prototypes | `concepts/a-living-tree/`, `concepts/b-forest-monument/`, `concepts/c-forest-system/` |
| Shared helper | `concepts/shared/concept.{js,css}` (timeline, scroll scenes, labels) |
| Category-B logo proposal asset | `concepts/shared/assets/proposal-tree-mark-{600,1161}.webp` |
| Review page | `concepts/index.html` |
| Screenshot boards | `concepts/review/` |

The review boards are:

- `hero-comparison.jpg`: A | B | C, desktop and mobile;
- `{A,B,C}-intro-keyframes.jpg`: six intro frames per direction;
- `{A,B,C}-scroll-scenes.jpg`: hero, scenes 1–3 and page end.

All screenshots are real captures of the prototypes in Chromium (Playwright),
not mock-ups.

## 0. Ground rules applied to all three

### Logo

**Category A: the authentic logo, unchanged.** All three directions show the
original emblem PNG (`logo-192/384.webp`, derived from the live site's file)
top-left, unaltered. On dark grounds it sits on a paper plate (ring contrast
2.06:1 otherwise).

- No direction animates the authentic logo beyond its appearance on the page.
- The flat PNG has no separate branches or leaves. Any "growing logo" would be
  a redraw, so none is claimed.
- The Stage 04 masked reveal (`INTRO_PROTOTYPE.md`) remains the reference for
  what motion on the authentic PNG can honestly do.

**Category B: proposals that need approval.** Each is visibly labelled on the
page with a dashed "Vorschlag B" tag.

| Where | Proposal | How it is made |
|---|---|---|
| B | Relief treatment of the emblem ("nicht das offizielle Logo") | Shading computed from the original PNG's alpha channel. A visual effect on the original, not a redraw. No lettering is invented or changed |
| C | Lockup "tree-mark + Baum-Land-Forst" ("Baum-Zeichen aus dem Original-Emblem") | Inner disc (r < 580 px) cut from the original PNG, pixel-identical (verified). The word mark is typeset in Newsreader; it is a proposal, not existing artwork |

**The tree in Direction A is an artwork, not the logo.** It is generated by
code, inspired by the emblem's tree, and never presented as the logo.

The tiny amber lettering of the emblem is never the visual centre in any
direction.

### Content

- Only verified text is used:
  - the service names;
  - the "Wir bieten Ihnen …" sentence;
  - the Kronensicherung sentence;
  - the contact question, phone, e-mail and address.
- No years, team, certificates, testimonials, awards, guarantees or prices.
- No owner name (D-28 unchanged).

### Photos

| Rule | Status |
|---|---|
| Photos used | Only real company photos: GAL-02, 04, 06, 11, 13, 14, 18, 19 |
| Excluded photos | GAL-03, GAL-15 (D-12) and GAL-16 (D-14) are not used |
| Before/after pairs | None |
| Upscaling | Checked at 1440 px @1× and @2×, and 390 px @2× and @3×: no image is shown at more than 1 device px per source px |

### Intro

- Every direction:
  - runs about 3 s once on load;
  - has no loading screen and no video;
  - does not block clicks;
  - keeps contact (phone + Anfrage) visible from the first frame.
- With `prefers-reduced-motion` or without JavaScript, the page renders the
  end state as a normal stacked page. Verified with `?rm=1` captures.

### Higgsfield

Higgsfield was **not used**. It spends account credits, and no budget was
given. More importantly, every visual here is code-rendered or a real photo,
so no generated image could be mistaken for company work.

## A · Lebender Baum (Living Tree)

**Thesis.** The site begins the way the company's work begins: with a tree,
growing in real time, until the light finds it.

**Emotional impression.** Calm, alive, hopeful. Organic care rather than
machinery.

### Art direction and colour

- Deep forest ground: radial forest-700 → forest-950.
- Lime-400 and its tints only in the leaves.
- Harz-500 for the primary action.
- The light is a warm lime glow from the upper right.
- Three depth layers: a sharp main tree and two dim background trees at
  18–22 % opacity, plus ground haze.

### Typography and composition

- Newsreader H1 on the left third; the main tree is off-centre at 66 % width.
- H1 "Baumfällung, Baumpflege, Baumkontrolle." (verified service names).
- Hanken Grotesk body text.

### Intro, frame by frame (3.2 s)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–0.25 s | Dark ground; the background trees begin |
| 0.25–1.6 s | The main trunk grows, then branches level by level (seeded, so identical every visit) |
| up to ≈ 2.1 s | Leaves open per canopy sector as their twigs complete, never before the twig exists |
| 1.7–2.6 s | Light arrives from the upper right; lit leaves turn brighter and a rim light grazes the main limbs |
| 2.3–3.0 s | Label, H1 and body text rise in (16 px) and fade in |

On desktop the three layers then drift slightly with the pointer.

### Scroll narrative (300 vh sticky stage)

1. **Scene 1 (0–33 %).** The camera pushes into the canopy (canvas scale
   1 → 2.6) and the hero text leaves.
2. **Scene 2 (33–68 %).** A real photo enters from the canopy: GAL-13 (crown
   bracing) with the verified Kronensicherung sentence. This is the bridge
   from metaphor to real work.
3. **Scene 3 (70–85 %).** "Vom Baum bis zur Fläche": the four service groups.
   Then come a normal "Echte Arbeit, echte Fotos" section (GAL-04, 11, 18, 14)
   and contact.

**Desktop/mobile.** Mobile shows only the main tree at depth 7, with no
pointer parallax.

**Accessibility and reduced motion.** With reduced motion, the end state is
drawn once and the page stacks normally. The canvas is `aria-hidden`; all
content is HTML.

### Technical strategy

- Canvas 2D, about 5.6 kB of tree code, no dependencies.
- Fallback: static stacked page.

**Risk (measured).** In headless Chromium with 4× CPU throttling, the intro
drops to p50 33 ms / p95 > 400 ms per frame. At 1×, p95 is 67 ms. The cause
is that every leaf is redrawn every frame.

**Mitigation, not built yet.**

- Bake finished branches into an offscreen layer.
- Draw only the growing tips.
- Cap the leaf count on mobile.

Effort: 1–2 days.

### Assessment

**Pros**

- The most emotional option.
- Directly echoes the emblem's tree without touching it.
- The canopy zoom into a real crown photo is a strong bridge.

**Cons**

- Generative trees are a known genre, so the risk of looking like a demo
  is real.
- The canopy is still too dome-shaped (needs irregular, asymmetric growth).
- The dark ground departs from the paper-led Revier system.
- It is the heaviest at runtime.

**Brand fit.** High in meaning, medium in tone: dark and dramatic for a
regional service company.

**Award potential.** Only if the tree is genuinely beautiful (botanical
accuracy, real species character such as a pine) and the zoom into the photo
is seamless. That is the hard part.

## B · Wald-Monument (Forest Monument)

**Thesis.** The company name as a monument: three words cut from bark,
revealed by light.

**Emotional impression.** Weight, permanence, craft. Bold and confident,
almost architectural.

### Art direction and colour

- Near-black ground (#0b0d0a).
- Procedural bark texture inside the letters (warm browns, no photo).
- Paper raking light.
- Harz-500 for the action; lime is barely used.

### Typography and composition

- "BAUM LAND FORST" in Newsreader. The widest word is measured to 68 % of the
  width (94 % on mobile).
- The copy sits in a right-hand column, with the category-B relief proposal
  above it.

### Intro, frame by frame (3.0 s)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–0.4 s | Black |
| 0.4–0.9 s | A vertical light slit opens in the centre |
| 0.9–1.7 s | The slit widens into a column and reveals the bark letters |
| 1.6–2.4 s | A raking light sweeps across the bark |
| 2.2–2.7 s | The relief proposal fades in |
| 2.5–3.0 s | The copy fades in |

### Scroll narrative

1. **Scene 1 (5–35 %).** The words split apart vertically (±25 % height). The
   gap opens the way forward.
2. **Scene 2 (30–68 %).** Photo plinths: GAL-04 and GAL-11 stand like
   exhibits, each with a verified service caption.
3. **Scene 3 (68 %+).** "Leistungsregister": the ten services as a numbered
   index (01–10). Then contact.

**Desktop/mobile.** Mobile stacks the words larger and puts the copy below
them.

**Accessibility and reduced motion.** The words are in a canvas, so the H1
carries the four service-group names (Stage 02 sitemap labels) as real text. With reduced motion, the
page renders static and stacked.

### Technical strategy

- Canvas 2D: the bark is generated once per resize; the text mask uses
  `source-in`.
- Low risk.
- Measured p95 at 4× throttling: 167 ms (desktop) and 83 ms (mobile) during
  the sweep.
- Fix: pre-render the light band. Effort: 1 day.

### Assessment

**Pros**

- The highest instant impact.
- Unmistakable typography.
- Cheap to build.

**Cons**

- The bark is synthetic. A real bark photo would be better and would need a
  company photo.
- It is mostly about the name, and says little about the work.
- The dark, monumental tone suits a museum more than a local service
  company.
- The relief treatment of the logo is the most debatable category-B item.

**Brand fit.** Low to medium.

**Award potential.** High for typography juries; low for "experience"
juries.

## C · Wald als System (The Forest as a System)

**Thesis.** One tree's rings become the map of everything the company does:
growth rings turn into contour lines, and the services sit on them.

**Emotional impression.** Clear, intelligent, calm, precise: the forest as a
managed system, which is what the company actually offers.

### Art direction and colour

- Paper ground (Revier paper-50).
- Forest-600 tree rings; bark-500 contour lines.
- Harz-700 for the group names, and an amber marker at the centre.
- Photos appear in circular "core" windows.

### Typography and composition

- Newsreader H1 on the left, over a paper scrim so no line crosses the text.
- The ring system sits at 64 % width.
- The service names are set on the rings (SVG `textPath`): spatial
  typography that is real, selectable text.

### Intro, frame by frame (3.2 s)

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0.2–0.5 s | An amber marker appears at the centre: the pith |
| 0.5–1.9 s | 14 tree rings draw outward, one after another |
| 1.2–2.6 s | 12 wider, wobblier contour lines continue beyond them: the tree becomes the landscape |
| 1.8–2.4 s | The ten service names fade in along the rings (the four groups on mobile) |
| 2.2–2.8 s | The centre opens as a circular window onto GAL-13, a real crown photo |
| 2.6–3.2 s | The copy fades in |

### Scroll narrative

1. **Scene 1 (2–32 %).** The ring system turns −14° and steps back; the
   window shrinks.
2. **Scene 2 (34–70 %).** A node map of the four service groups. GAL-02, a
   typographic "02" node for Grün & Forst (no photo exists, D-19), GAL-06 and
   GAL-14.
3. **Scene 3 (68 %+).** "Querschnitt: echte Arbeit", an editorial spread with
   GAL-04, 11, 18 and 19. Then contact.

**Desktop/mobile.**

- Mobile puts the rings on the upper 60 % and the copy below on paper.
- Nine rings, and four group labels on short arcs that never pass the screen
  edge.

**Accessibility and reduced motion.**

- All text is HTML or SVG text.
- Reduced motion and no-JS give a stacked page: the rings as an image, then
  the copy, nodes and spread (verified).

### Technical strategy

- SVG with stroke-dash drawing; no canvas, no dependencies.
- Measured 60 fps (p95 16.8 ms) even at 4× throttling, on desktop and
  mobile.
- Lowest risk.

### Assessment

**Pros**

- Uses real photos from the first second.
- Turns the service list into the visual idea itself.
- Fits the approved paper-led Revier system.
- Scales naturally to the Leistungen page (rings → service groups) and the
  gallery.
- The fastest of the three.

**Cons**

- Less immediate drama than B.
- The idea needs the scroll to unfold; the hero alone reads as "elegant",
  not "wow".
- Scene 2 (node map) is still a rough layout.

**Brand fit.** High: precise, regional, about the work.

**Award potential.** It needs crafted detail to win:

- ring shapes from a real tree cross-section (a scan or the company's own
  photo, if one exists);
- smooth morphing from rings to the node map;
- sound-free micro-interaction on the labels.

## Comparison and recommendation

Scores 1–5, honest judgement after viewing the real prototypes.

| Criterion | A · Lebender Baum | B · Wald-Monument | C · Wald als System |
|---|---|---|---|
| Originality | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| Visual impact | 4 | 5 | 3 |
| Brand fit | 4 | 2 | 4 |
| Storytelling | 4 | 3 | 5 |
| Usability | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| Technical feasibility | 3 | 4 | 5 |
| Performance | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| Distinctiveness | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| **Total (of 40)** | **26** | **28** | **34** |

**Recommendation: C, "Wald als System"**, with two upgrades before it goes
further:

1. **Raise the hero's impact.** Use a larger, bolder ring field, and more
   contrast for the window photo.
2. **Borrow A's best moment.** The light reaching the real photo, used once
   when the window opens.

Why C wins:

- It turns the company's real breadth into the visual idea.
- It keeps real photographs central.
- It fits the approved Revier tokens.
- It is the only direction that is already fast on low-end devices.

B is the strongest poster but the weakest website. A is the most emotional,
but carries the highest risk of looking generic and the highest runtime cost.

### Technical realism

- **Three.js/WebGL is not justified** for any of the three.
  - A: Canvas 2D is enough for a growing tree. WebGL would only be warranted
    for true 3D foliage with lighting, which this brief does not need.
  - B: the bark and the light are 2D.
  - C: the rings are vector.
  - Adding Three.js (≈ 150 kB gzip) would add weight without a visible gain.
- **Dependencies added:** none. The prototypes are plain HTML/CSS/JS, like
  `src/`.
- **Slow connections.** Every intro runs on code alone (about 20–25 kB of
  uncompressed HTML/JS per concept, plus fonts); photos load progressively and are not required
  for the intro. C's window shows paper until GAL-13 arrives.

| | A | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Effort to production quality (Home only) | 6–8 days | 4–5 days | 5–6 days |
| Highest risk | Frame rate and "generic generative tree" look | Tone: dark and monumental vs. local service; synthetic bark | Hero not striking enough without more craft |
| Fallback | Static end frame, stacked page | Static words, stacked page | Static rings, stacked page |

## Decisions needed from the project owner

1. **Choose the direction:** A, B or C (recommended: C).
2. **Category-B logo proposals:** approve or reject each one. Approval means
   the owner (and ideally the original designer) agrees:
   - the C lockup, a tree-mark cut from the emblem plus the typeset
     "Baum-Land-Forst";
   - the B relief shading of the emblem.

   Until approved, only the unchanged emblem may appear as the logo.
3. **Original logo files:** does a vector or layered original exist? Without
   one, no motion beyond the masked reveal of the PNG is possible honestly.
4. **Dark grounds (A, B):** these depart from the approved paper-led Revier
   system. Accept, or keep paper (C)?
5. **Headline wording.** C's "Zehn Leistungen, ein Betrieb." counts the ten
   services listed on the live site. Confirm that the count, and the
   implication that all are done in-house, are correct.
6. **Real tree cross-section (C) or bark (B):** does the company have its own
   photo? Generated images will not be used as company work.
7. **Unchanged open items:**
   - D-28: the owner name; neither name is used;
   - D-12: GAL-03 and GAL-15 permissions;
   - D-14: GAL-16 excluded;
   - D-08: claims.

| Add to a concept URL | Effect |
|---|---|

## Known limitations

- Tested in Chromium only (Playwright). Safari and Firefox are not tested.
- The frame times above are from headless Chromium with software rendering
  and CPU throttling. They are indicative, not device measurements.
- No contrast or keyboard audit has been run on the concepts. They are
  direction prototypes, and the Stage 04 QA script covers `src/` only.
- Horizontal overflow checked: 0 px at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px for all
  three.
