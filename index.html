02 — Approach

Most desks in Dubai work on volume. We work the other way: a short list, walked personally, with the paperwork checked before a single viewing is arranged.

03 — Process

Four steps, no surprises

  1. 01

    Brief

    A call to set the budget, the district and the timeline. Thirty minutes, no deck.

  2. 02

    Shortlist

    Three addresses, each with title status, service charge and a note on what is wrong with it.

  3. 03

    Viewing

    Private access, out of hours where the building allows it. We attend with you.

  4. 04

    Transfer

    Offer, escrow and DLD transfer handled with your counsel. You sign, we run the file.
